# FiguredIt growth checklist

Based on the [Ship It Growth Guide](https://shipit-student-setup.build989979.chatgpt.site/growth/). Treat the guide's traffic and revenue targets as experiments, not promises. Do not publish fabricated customers, outcomes, or testimonials.

## Completed in the product

- Clear B2B ICP positioning, one public demo, visible Free/Starter/Pro pricing, and a single signup path already exist.
- Public pages have titles and canonical URLs; `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` exist. A practical ICP guide with a fictional worked example is now linked from the site.
- Simple Analytics tracks public pages and the existing signup, upload, analysis, export, waitlist, and pricing events. Do not attach names, email addresses, or uploaded rows to these events.
- On 28 October 2026, the `https://figuredit.in/` URL-prefix property was verified in Google Search Console with the live HTML verification file. Google accepted `sitemap.xml` (9 discovered pages), and indexing was requested for the ICP guide.
- The verified property and its sitemap were imported into Bing Webmaster Tools. Bing shows one known sitemap with no errors or warnings; crawling and URL discovery are still processing.

## Owner steps, in order

1. **Customer evidence:** Ask five B2B founders or sales leads to try one real lead-list task. Record the first blocker, whether they reached a useful result, and the wording they used for the problem. Ask permission before publishing any quote or case study.
2. **Content:** The first worked [ICP guide](https://figuredit.in/guides/ideal-customer-profile-from-leads) is live. Test these five *keyword hypotheses* against actual customer wording and Search Console queries before writing another page: "ideal customer profile from lead list", "how to score B2B companies", "B2B lead scoring from CSV", "prioritize sales accounts by fit", "ICP analysis for SaaS founders". These are ideas, not measured search volumes.
3. **Outreach:** If target buyers use X or LinkedIn, share the fictional guide and [keyword preview](https://figuredit.in/demo) with a clear sample-data label. Draft: "Have a CSV of B2B accounts but no clear way to choose who to contact first? FiguredIt groups accounts by company, uses AI-assisted analysis, and gives an explainable score. The public demo is a keyword preview with fictional data; the Free plan accepts a 25-row CSV and analyzes up to 25 companies. Try the preview: https://figuredit.in/demo. If you work through a lead list, what is the hardest part of deciding who to pursue?" Do not claim proven uplift or customer results without evidence.
4. **Distribution shortlist:** [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch) (maker audience; prepare a real demo and two gallery images), [SaaSHub](https://www.saashub.com/services/submit) (software discovery), [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/faq/) (software alternatives; verified email required), [BetaList](https://betalist.com/criteria) (only if still recently launched; check paid submission terms), and [Startup India Showcase](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/sis.html) (only if eligible and able to supply the required DIPP number and pitch materials). Check each audience and current rules before submitting; record actual approval and referrals, not just form submissions.
5. **Review weekly:** Compare public visits by source, signup completion, first useful analysis, return usage, and operating cost. Fix the most repeated user blocker before increasing outreach or paid spend. Keep existing prices until actual conversion and cost data support a change. Recheck Google and Bing sitemap/indexing statuses after their processing window.
